About

Hello, I’m JooYoung Roh, a conductor exploring the intersection of music and technology.

Based on my experience in orchestral and choral conducting, as well as performance, I have learned how music connects people and brings individual voices together into a single resonance. Through conducting, I find deep fulfillment in understanding the structure and flow of a piece, and in shaping its musical message together with performers.

I believe music is not only an art of emotion and intuition, but also a field deeply connected to engineering concepts such as structure, pattern, proportion, acoustics, and logic. From this perspective, I continue to explore ways of interpreting music through different lenses and communicating it with greater clarity and persuasion.

Alongside my musical work, I also draw on my professional background in software development to create and use tools for musical practice and production. I am particularly interested in using AI to analyze countless possibilities in reharmonization and to formalize recurring musical choices and patterns. Furthermore, I explore new approaches to music creation by converting musical elements such as notes, durations, and harmonic progressions into data, modeling structured compositional techniques, and orchestrating multiple AI models according to their intended purposes.

I hope to continue exploring the meeting point between music and technology, communicating more maturely with performers, and becoming a conductor who shares comfort and hope through music.

Now, let’s begin this journey together.
